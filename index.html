🚫 Can't Measure — Enter Estimated Time Instead
Use when the property cannot be accurately measured. Staff enter estimated mow time directly — pricing will be calculated from time, not square footage.
Enter total estimated mow time for a solo crew member
⚠️ Manual time bypasses square footage. Mowing price is calculated directly from your entered minutes × active hourly rate × difficulty multiplier. Area will show as "Est. (manual)" in the estimate.
📐 Enter Known Lawn Area: sq ft or acres (e.g. LotScope)
Use when you have an accurate lawn measurement from LotScope or another tool. If it gives acreage, enter the acres and the square feet fill in for you. Pricing is calculated from square footage using standard rate tables.
Enter total mowable lawn area in square feet
1 acre = 43,560 sq ft. LotScope rounds acres, so if it also shows square feet, enter that instead for a tighter number.
📐 Square footage entry uses standard mowing rate tables. Select service type and difficulty in Step 3 to complete the estimate.
